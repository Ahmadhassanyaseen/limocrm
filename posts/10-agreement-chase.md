## Problem

PDF in email, DocuSign login, “did they sign yet?” in the group chat.

Sales chases signatures across tools. Dispatch never knows which jobs are actually closed.

## Solution

Staff generate a signing link from CRM. The customer reviews the trip, e-signs, and pays on a branded public page. Staff see what’s still awaiting signature.

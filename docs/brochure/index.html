01

Command center

Start every day knowing pipeline health and performance

Dashboard

See year-to-date lead volume, open pipeline, and wins in charts and KPI cards at a glance. Owners get a team-wide view; staff see only the pipeline they own, so everyone starts the day knowing what needs attention.

  • YTD lead charts and monthly win tracking
  • Open pipeline KPI cards at a glance
  • Admin vs staff pipeline visibility by role
  • Quick health check before the daily standup

Reports & Analytics

Drill into performance by month, quarter, or year with funnel stages, geographic breakdowns, and revenue-style metrics drawn from real lead data. Use it to spot which routes, channels, and periods drive growth—and where deals stall.

  • Funnel and stage analysis by month, quarter, or year
  • Geographic breakdown from pickup and dropoff data
  • Revenue-style metrics from trip pricing fields
  • Trend lines to spot seasonal peaks and slow periods
02

Sales pipeline

From first inquiry to won booking in one workspace

Leads

Every inquiry lives in one searchable list with status, assignment, trip details, and pricing. It is the central hub for moving a job from first contact to a won booking.

  • Searchable list with status, assignee, and trip details
  • Pickup, dropoff, date, and price on every row
  • Role-based create, edit, and delete permissions
  • Central hub from new inquiry to won booking

Lead detail

Open a single lead to see full history, related emails, and actions to send a formal quote or agreement link. Your team stays in one workspace instead of jumping between inbox, docs, and payment tools.

  • Full trip history and pricing on one screen
  • Related emails tied to the lead timeline
  • Send quote or agreement links from the record
  • Status updates without leaving the workspace

Add / Edit lead

Capture new bookings or update existing ones with forms built for limo trips—pickup, dropoff, date, passengers, vehicle, and assignee. Keeps data clean from the first touch so quotes and agreements are accurate.

  • Limo-specific fields: pickup, dropoff, date, passengers
  • Vehicle and assignee selection on every job
  • Clean data from first touch for accurate quotes
  • Edit existing records without re-entering everything

Contacts

Store long-term customer records separate from one-off leads, with detail and edit views for repeat clients and corporate accounts. Builds a relationship layer that supports upsell and loyalty over time.

  • Long-term customer records separate from one-off leads
  • Detail and edit views for repeat clients
  • Corporate account tracking for B2B sales
  • Relationship layer for upsell and loyalty

Notes

Attach internal notes to CRM records so dispatch, sales, and owners share context in one place. Replaces scattered email threads and sticky notes when handoffs happen.

  • Internal notes attached to CRM records
  • Shared context for dispatch, sales, and owners
  • Replaces scattered email threads on handoffs
  • Timestamped history on each record

Tasks

Track follow-up to-dos tied to CRM work—calls, quotes, and check-ins—so nothing slips after a conversation. Keeps the team accountable without a separate task app.

  • Follow-up to-dos tied to CRM records
  • Calls, quotes, and check-ins tracked in one list
  • Nothing slips after a customer conversation
  • No separate task app required
03

Fleet & pricing

Quote from the vehicles and rates you actually run

Vehicles (Fleet)

Maintain a catalog of limos with capacity, features, photos, and status in a fleet grid. Sales always quotes from what you actually run, not outdated spreadsheets.

  • Fleet grid with capacity, features, and photos
  • Active/inactive status per vehicle
  • Sales quotes from live fleet data
  • Replaces outdated spreadsheet catalogs

Vehicle detail

View deep specs, image gallery, and pricing for one vehicle when configuring quotes or reviewing fleet ops. Gives dispatch and sales the same source of truth on each unit.

  • Deep specs and image gallery per vehicle
  • Per-vehicle pricing overrides
  • Same source of truth for dispatch and sales
  • Quick review before quoting a specific unit

Pricing (Admin)

Set global hourly rates, fuel surcharge, and driver commission defaults, then override per vehicle when needed. Protects margin while keeping quotes consistent across the team.

  • Global hourly rates and fuel surcharge defaults
  • Driver commission settings at account level
  • Per-vehicle pricing overrides when needed
  • Consistent quotes that protect margin
04

Revenue: agreements & payments

Sign, collect, and reconcile without leaving CRM

Agreements (staff)

Manage agreement records and generate signing links from inside CRM for jobs ready to close. Track which bookings are still awaiting signature so sales can follow up quickly.

  • Agreement records linked to leads
  • Generate signing links from inside CRM
  • Track bookings awaiting signature
  • Fast follow-up when deals are ready to close

Public agreement page

Customers open a branded page to review the trip, sign electronically, and pay via Stripe, PayPal, or offline—without staff in the loop. Shortens the gap between “yes” and money in the bank.

  • Branded page with full trip details
  • Electronic signature without staff involvement
  • Stripe, PayPal, or offline payment options
  • Shorter gap between “yes” and payment

Payment methods (Admin)

Connect Stripe and PayPal and choose the default payment mode for each operator account. Each owner controls how their customers pay while staying inside one platform.

  • Connect Stripe and PayPal per account
  • Choose default payment mode for customers
  • Owner-controlled payment configuration
  • All payment setup inside one platform

Transactions

View a ledger of payments linked to jobs for reconciliation and cash-flow visibility. Owners see what cleared, when, and against which booking.

  • Payment ledger linked to each job
  • Reconciliation and cash-flow visibility
  • See what cleared and when
  • Match payments to specific bookings

Communications

Professional email from your brand, with measurable results

Email settings

Configure outbound SMTP accounts so quotes and agreements send from your domain and brand. Professional delivery builds trust and keeps messages out of spam folders.

  • Outbound SMTP from your domain
  • Branded sender for quotes and agreements
  • Professional delivery builds customer trust
  • Better deliverability vs generic senders

Email templates

Build and edit templates with a visual editor and LimoCRM placeholders for tracking and unsubscribe links. Every outreach looks consistent—formal quotes, agreements, and follow-ups included.

  • Visual editor with LimoCRM placeholders
  • Tracking and unsubscribe links built in
  • Consistent quotes, agreements, and follow-ups
  • Reusable templates across the team

Email analytics

See how outbound mail performs with open and engagement metrics per user. Refine templates and timing based on data, not guesswork.

  • Open and engagement metrics per user
  • Performance data for outbound mail
  • Refine templates based on real results
  • Optimize send timing with data

Email detail

Open a single sent message from a lead’s timeline for support, disputes, or audit. Full context on what went out and when, tied to the job.

  • Single sent message from the lead timeline
  • Full context for support and disputes
  • Audit trail of what went out and when
  • Tied directly to the job record

Growth & automation

Capture demand and automate repetitive work

Integrations (Widget)

Get embed code, live preview, and controls for accent color and font to match your brand on any website. See which domains drive leads so marketing spend ties back to CRM data.

  • Embed code with live preview
  • Accent color and font match your brand
  • Track which domains drive leads
  • Marketing spend tied back to CRM data

Instant quote widget

Visitors enter pickup, destination, date, and passengers, pick a vehicle, and submit—creating a new lead in CRM automatically. Turns passive site traffic into qualified pipeline without phone tag.

  • Pickup, destination, date, and passenger fields
  • Vehicle selection before submit
  • Auto-creates a new lead in CRM
  • Turns site traffic into qualified pipeline

Workflows

Define rule-based automation with conditions and actions on leads and contacts—status changes, follow-ups, and repetitive updates. Frees staff from manual clicks as volume grows.

  • Rule-based conditions and actions
  • Automate status changes and follow-ups
  • Works on leads and contacts
  • Frees staff from manual clicks at scale

Welcome / OTP funnel

Prospects arrive via a marketing link, verify their email with a one-time code, and enter CRM securely. Campaign-driven growth stays verified and traceable from first click.

  • Marketing links with verified email entry
  • One-time code (OTP) for secure access
  • Campaign-driven growth stays traceable
  • Verified prospects from first click

Explore demo login

Campaign links with tracking IDs can auto-open a demo workspace so prospects evaluate the product immediately. Sales and marketing see which sends convert to product exploration.

  • Campaign links with tracking IDs
  • Auto-open demo workspace for prospects
  • Immediate product evaluation
  • Track which sends convert to exploration

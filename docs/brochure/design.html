01

Command center

Start every day knowing pipeline health and performance

Module 01.1

Dashboard

See year-to-date lead volume, open pipeline, and wins in charts and KPI cards at a glance. Owners get a team-wide view; staff see only the pipeline they own, so everyone starts the day knowing what needs attention.

  • YTD lead charts and monthly win tracking
  • Open pipeline KPI cards at a glance
  • Admin vs. staff pipeline visibility by role
  • Quick health check before the daily standup
Module 01.2

Reports & Analytics

Drill into performance by month, quarter, or year with funnel stages, geographic breakdowns, and revenue-style metrics drawn from real lead data. Use it to spot which routes, channels, and periods drive growth—and where deals stall.

  • Funnel and stage analysis by month, quarter, or year
  • Geographic breakdown from pickup and dropoff data
  • Revenue-style metrics from trip pricing fields
  • Trend lines to spot seasonal peaks and slow periods
02

Sales pipeline

Work every inquiry to a closed deal

Module 02.1

Leads

A filterable, sortable table of every inquiry with status, source, trip date, and assigned rep. Bulk-assign, search by name or company, and jump straight into the detail view—all from one screen that keeps the whole team aligned.

  • Filterable and sortable table across all reps
  • Status, source, trip date, and assignee at a glance
  • Bulk-assign leads without opening each record
  • One-click into full lead detail
Module 02.2

Lead detail

Every field, note, email, task, and agreement for a single lead on one scrollable page. No switching tabs—reps get full context from first contact to closed deal, with a timeline that shows exactly what happened and when.

  • Full trip details, pricing, and status on one page
  • Activity timeline — notes, emails, status changes
  • Linked tasks and agreements in the same view
  • Send email and update status without leaving the record
Module 02.3

Contacts

A deduplicated directory of everyone your team has ever interacted with. Search by name or company, review all linked leads for a person, and keep notes on corporate and repeat clients — separate from individual trip records.

  • Deduplicated directory across all reps
  • View every lead linked to a contact
  • Notes for corporate and repeat client context
  • Fast search by name or company
Module 02.4

Tasks

Create, assign, and track follow-up tasks tied to leads or contacts. Filter by due date and assignee, and close tasks from the task list or directly from the lead record so nothing falls through the cracks.

  • Tasks tied to leads or contacts
  • Assign to any team member with due dates
  • Filter and sort by due date or assignee
  • Close tasks from the list or the lead record
03

Fleet & pricing

Keep the catalog current and profitable

Module 03.1

Vehicles

Manage the full fleet with photos, capacity, and status so the instant quote widget always shows what's actually available. Add or retire vehicles in seconds — the catalog stays live across the CRM and the customer-facing widget simultaneously.

  • Full fleet catalog with photos and capacity
  • Active / inactive status synced to the widget
  • Add or retire vehicles in seconds
  • Live across CRM and quote widget simultaneously
Module 03.2

Pricing

Set base rates, per-mile and per-hour rules, and flat-rate overrides per vehicle so every quote is calculated consistently. Reps always quote the same number for the same trip — no spreadsheets, no guesswork, no margin erosion.

  • Base, per-mile, and per-hour rate rules per vehicle
  • Flat-rate overrides for recurring routes
  • Consistent quotes across the whole team
  • No spreadsheets or manual calculations
04

Agreements & payments

Close and collect in one link

Module 04.1

Agreements

Generate a booking agreement pre-filled from the lead record, send a secure link, and get a signed copy back — all without leaving CRM. Signed agreements attach to the lead timeline automatically so the audit trail is always complete.

  • Pre-filled from the lead record in one click
  • Secure signing link sent to the client
  • Signed copy auto-attached to the lead timeline
  • Full audit trail from send to signature
Module 04.2

Transactions

Stripe-powered payment collection tied directly to the agreement and lead. Clients pay from the same secure link they signed — no separate invoicing tool required. Payment status updates the lead automatically the moment funds clear.

  • Stripe-powered collection linked to the agreement
  • Client pays from the same link they signed
  • No separate invoicing tool required
  • Lead status updates automatically on payment
05

Email communications

On-brand outreach with real metrics

Module 05.1

Email templates

Build and reuse branded email templates with LimoCRM placeholders that auto-fill from the lead record. Quotes, agreements, and follow-ups all go out consistently—every time, from every rep—without anyone writing from scratch.

  • Visual editor with LimoCRM placeholders
  • Tracking and unsubscribe links built in
  • Consistent quotes, agreements, and follow-ups
  • Reusable templates across the team
Module 05.2

Email analytics

See how outbound mail performs with open and engagement metrics per user. Refine templates and timing based on data — not guesswork — and identify which messages drive replies versus which go cold.

  • Open and engagement metrics per user
  • Performance data for all outbound mail
  • Refine templates based on real results
  • Optimize send timing with data
Module 05.3

Email detail

Open a single sent message from a lead's timeline for support, disputes, or audit. Full context on what went out and when, tied to the job record so the whole story is there when a client calls in.

  • Single sent message from the lead timeline
  • Full context for support and disputes
  • Audit trail of what went out and when
  • Tied directly to the job record
06

Growth & automation

Capture demand and automate repetitive work

Module 06.1

Integrations (Widget)

Get embed code, live preview, and controls for accent color and font to match your brand on any website. See which domains drive leads so marketing spend ties back to CRM data.

  • Embed code with live preview
  • Accent color and font match your brand
  • Track which domains drive leads
  • Marketing spend tied back to CRM data
Module 06.2

Instant quote widget

Visitors enter pickup, destination, date, and passengers, pick a vehicle, and submit — creating a new lead in CRM automatically. Turns passive site traffic into qualified pipeline without phone tag.

  • Pickup, destination, date, and passenger fields
  • Vehicle selection before submit
  • Auto-creates a new lead in CRM
  • Turns site traffic into qualified pipeline
Module 06.3

Workflows

Define rule-based automation with conditions and actions on leads and contacts — status changes, follow-ups, and repetitive updates. Frees staff from manual clicks as volume grows.

  • Rule-based conditions and actions
  • Automate status changes and follow-ups
  • Works on leads and contacts
  • Frees staff from manual clicks at scale
Module 06.4

Welcome / OTP funnel

Prospects arrive via a marketing link, verify their email with a one-time code, and enter CRM securely. Campaign-driven growth stays verified and traceable from first click.

  • Marketing links with verified email entry
  • One-time code (OTP) for secure access
  • Campaign-driven growth stays traceable
  • Verified prospects from first click
Module 06.5

Explore demo login

Campaign links with tracking IDs can auto-open a demo workspace so prospects evaluate the product immediately. Sales and marketing see which sends convert to product exploration.

  • Campaign links with tracking IDs
  • Auto-open demo workspace for prospects
  • Immediate product evaluation
  • Track which sends convert to exploration
07

Administration & account

Scale the team without losing control

Module 07.1

Users

Add team members, assign roles, and manage active accounts as the company scales. Onboarding new hires takes minutes, not an IT project.

  • Add team members and assign roles
  • Manage active and inactive accounts
  • Onboarding in minutes, not an IT project
  • Scale the team as the company grows
Module 07.2

Role management

Build roles with module-level create, read, update, and delete permissions — sales sees leads, not payment keys. Owners keep control; staff see only what their job requires.

  • Module-level CRUD permissions
  • Sales sees leads, not payment keys
  • Custom roles for each job function
  • Owners keep control as the team scales
Module 07.3

Settings

Update profile details, username, phone, and password in one account screen. Self-service changes reduce support load on admins.

  • Profile, username, phone, and password in one screen
  • Self-service account updates
  • Reduces admin support load
  • Quick changes without IT help
Module 07.4

Profile

Quick view of the signed-in user's identity and shortcuts to account actions. Keeps personal info accessible from anywhere in the app.

  • Signed-in user identity at a glance
  • Shortcuts to account actions
  • Accessible from anywhere in the app
  • Quick personal info without digging

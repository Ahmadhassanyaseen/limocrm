## Three pillars (instant quote · automation · low cost)

LimoCRM gives limousine operators three things they usually pay for separately—**instant quotes**, **automation**, and **affordable software**—in one platform. Your website embeds a branded booking widget so visitors get trip pricing and become leads without phone tag or manual data entry. Built-in **workflows** handle follow-ups, status changes, and repetitive tasks so a small team can run like a larger one. And because LimoCRM is purpose-built for limo businesses—not a bloated enterprise CRM—you get leads, fleet, agreements, payments, and email in one place **at a fraction of the cost** of stitching together multiple tools. More bookings, less busywork, lower overhead.

## Short version (3–4 sentences)

**LimoCRM** is an all-in-one operating system built for limousine companies—not generic CRM software. It connects **website inquiries, sales pipeline, fleet, quotes, agreements, and payments** in a single workspace so your team stops juggling spreadsheets, inboxes, and disconnected tools. An **embeddable booking widget** turns site traffic into qualified leads automatically, while **role-based access** keeps owners, dispatch, and sales aligned. The result: faster follow-up, fewer dropped bookings, and clearer visibility into what’s winning revenue.

## Email pitch (concise)

**Subject:** One platform to run leads, fleet, and payments for your limo business

Hi [Name],

Most limo operators lose revenue in the gaps—leads sit in email, quotes go out manually, agreements and payments happen outside the system, and nobody has a real-time view of the pipeline.

**LimoCRM fixes that** by putting the full journey in one place:

**Problems it solves**
- **Scattered leads** — Inquiries from your website, campaigns, and team are managed in one pipeline with status, assignment, and history.
- **Slow quote-to-booking** — Send formal quotes and agreement links from the lead record instead of rebuilding documents every time.
- **Payment friction** — Customers can review, sign, and pay (Stripe/PayPal/offline) on a dedicated agreement page; transactions stay tied to the job.
- **Fleet blind spots** — Vehicles, capacity, and pricing live alongside sales so quotes match what you can actually run.
- **Team chaos** — Roles and permissions control who sees leads, fleet, email, and admin tools—owners get the full picture; staff see only what they need.
- **No visibility** — Dashboard and reports show leads created, open pipeline, wins, and trends so you know what’s working month to month.

**How it helps growth**
- **More leads, less manual work** — Embed a branded **instant-quote widget** on your site; bookings flow straight into CRM with source tracking.
- **Higher conversion** — Faster follow-up, professional email templates, and a smooth sign-and-pay flow reduce drop-offs after the quote.
- **Smarter operations** — Email analytics, workflow automation, and centralized contacts/notes mean fewer missed touchpoints.
- **Scalable team** — Add users and roles as you grow without losing control of data or payments.

**In one line:** LimoCRM turns website visitors into signed, paid bookings—and gives you one dashboard to run the business.

Happy to walk you through a live demo on your workflows (leads → quote → agreement → payment).

Best,  
[Your name]
